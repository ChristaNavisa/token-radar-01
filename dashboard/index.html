Watchlist protocols, ranked by implied value

The watchlist is the floor: every protocol with at least $50M of TVL and no token on record. The early radar adds genuine pre-token protocols below that floor — no bridges, no liquid staking, no wrapped or staked products, the same rule the watchlist uses. Implied value is a model, not a promise — and it is priced per category, not at one flat multiple.

What the numbers mean

Implied value
TVL × the category's geometric-median market-cap-to-TVL ratio (shrunk toward the global ratio when the category has few live tokens), moved by the seven-day growth term and the maturity term. One value per row — the board ranks by it and nothing else competes with it.
Geometric median
The median of the log ratios, exponentiated. Ratios are log-normal, so the geometric median is the honest centre: one extreme ratio cannot drag it the way an arithmetic mean can.
Growth term
1 + 0.25 × sign(growth) × ln(1 + |growth| / 100), with growth clamped to −60%…+120%. A +120% week is worth 1.20×; a −60% week is worth 0.88×.
Maturity term
0.60 + min(age, 1095) / 1095 × 0.20. A protocol that has run three years with no token is worth 0.80× on age; a new one is worth 0.60×.
Farm verdict
Farm, Thin or Skip, from the break-even share: the farm plan's total cost divided by the implied value. The board is split into thirds by that share, so the verdicts are relative to this board, not to an absolute dollar figure.
Rank range
The central 80% of the ranks a row takes when the multiples are refit 200 times on resampled live sets. A narrow range means the rank is stable; a wide one means it depends on which live tokens happen to be in the sample.
Leave-one-out
Each live protocol is scored as if it had no token — its own ratio left out of its category's multiple and of the global median — and the predicted value is compared with the market cap it actually trades at. The check is a ranking check: Spearman rho and top-decile hit.
Walk-forward
The multiples are learned only from the live protocols listed before a cut date, and scored on the ones listed after it. Three cuts: 2023-07-01, 2024-01-01, 2024-07-01.
