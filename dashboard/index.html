What the numbers mean

Implied value
TVL × the category's trimmed-mean market-cap-to-TVL ratio (shrunk toward the global ratio when the category has few live tokens), nudged by a weak growth term and a weak maturity term. Capped at 0.4× TVL at the low end and 4× at the high end.
Launch odds
The share of peer protocols — same age band and same category — that already have a token, shrunk toward the global launch rate. A first-class column, not a multiplier: multiplying the value by the odds makes the ranking worse on the blind tests, so the board ranks by implied value and shows the odds beside it.
Expected value
Implied value × launch odds. The value you would expect if the protocol launched at the comps-implied price.
Farm verdict
Farm, Thin or Skip, from the break-even share: the farm plan's total cost divided by the implied value. The board is split into thirds by that share, so the verdicts are relative to this board, not to an absolute dollar figure.
Rank range
The central 80% of the ranks a row takes when the multiples are refit 200 times on resampled live sets. A narrow range means the rank is stable; a wide one means it depends on which live tokens happen to be in the sample.
Leave-one-out
Each live protocol is scored as if it had no token — its own ratio left out of its category's multiple and of the global median — and the predicted value is compared with the market cap it actually trades at. The check is a ranking check: Spearman rho and top-decile hit.
Walk-forward
The multiples are learned only from the live protocols listed before a cut date, and scored on the ones listed after it. Three cuts: 2023-07-01, 2024-01-01, 2024-07-01.
